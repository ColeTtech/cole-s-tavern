The Tale of Cole the Butcher

It's a tale as old as time and not so far from here. There was a boy who was named Cole and he grew to be a man. A man who ventured into the world unknowing what he'll do with his life. He sat upon a rock and pondered what his life would be. he didnt have any great ambitions as of yet. So he decided upon becoming a butcher and thus he left to be apprenticed with a butcher named Yansin in the city of Antler. He never really yearned to be a butcher. There was no great "ah HA!!!" monent. He thought to himself. "I want a job where it's physically demanding so I can stay in shape. As well as a job that'll always be needed"

Departure Chapter 1

His ice covered lashes whispered as he open his eyes. A billow of heat clouded his vision as he peered out of his sable sleeping bag. "I must be getting close" he thought to himself. "I cant take this cold anymore." Four days later he reached the City of Antler.
