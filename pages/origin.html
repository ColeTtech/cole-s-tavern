The Tale of Cole the Butcher

It's a tale as old as time and not so far from here. There was a boy who was named Cole and he grew to be a man. A man who ventured into the world unknowing what he'll do with his life. He sat upon a rock and pondered what his life would be. he didnt have any great ambitions as of yet. So he decided upon becoming a butcher and thus he left to be apprenticed with a butcher named Casey in the city of Kamloops
